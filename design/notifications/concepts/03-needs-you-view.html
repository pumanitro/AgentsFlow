Needs-you tab + banner

Next to “Chat” and “Routines” there is a third tab, “Needs you”. While something is waiting for you, a slim orange strip sits under the header on every screen and points to it — it cannot be closed, it only goes away once the job is done.

  • Where it lives A third tab in the top switch, plus a strip on every screen
  • How you answer On a page of its own: list, form, background
  • How it gets your attention The orange strip slides in and pushes the screen down

      How it would be built (for the engineer)

      All six designs share one pipeline: the agent calls a new notify tool on the existing peersflow server, the main process stores the notice in notifications.json and pushes snapshots to the window, and answers go back into the source chat as one message. Details and types: the shared story on the index page.

      What this design adds:

      • renderer/lib/ui-state.ts — the view union gains 'needsYou'; a third pill button in pages/index.tsx (orange text and outline while the count of open action notices is above 0; plain at 0, never hidden).
      • components/needs-you/NeedsYouView.tsx rendered in the same slot as RoutinesView (it replaces the whole body like Routines does; no peers sidebar, no SpawnBar), with children NeedsYouList (groups “Needs you” / “Earlier”), NeedsYouWork (file card with Open / Reveal in Finder via the open_file route, questions, to-do, Send answers, Dismiss) and NeedsYouContext (320 px: routine, this run's card, previous run, Open chat).
      • NeedsYouBanner.tsx — 32 px, bg-panel, orange left edge, no ✕; rendered right after <header> in index.tsx, session.tsx and preview.tsx as a normal flex child (pushes content, no fixed positioning). In session.tsx the counter is a chip in the header actions, because that header has no Chat | Routines pill.
      • info / warn notices render as one-line rows under the banner (Routines toast look): info removes itself after 6000 ms with a visible bar; warn waits for ✕.
      • Selection (which notice is open) is remembered per window in renderer/lib/needs-you-ui-state.ts, same pattern as routines-ui-state.ts. The banner's “Review ›” selects the oldest open action.
      • Dismiss is disabled until canDismiss(n) and answersSentAt are both true; its tooltip lists what is missing. Dismissed notices stay under “Earlier” (180-day retention from the shared store); readAt is set when the work panel first shows a notice.
      • Effort L: a new top-level view, a banner in three pages, and the list/work/context split.

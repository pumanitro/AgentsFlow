Toasts + sticky cards

Small notices pop up in the bottom-right corner, the way chat apps do. Quick news fades away by itself; anything that needs you stays as a card you work through one step at a time — and if you fold it away, a small orange pill keeps reminding you on every screen.

  • Where it lives Bottom-right corner, every screen
  • How you answer One step at a time inside the card
  • How it gets your attention Slides up, glows, then waits

Try it

Press the steps, or use the card yourself. You can also click Chat / Routines inside the window or “Open chat ›” on the card — the card follows you to every screen.

You can see it from everywhere

Folded away, the card becomes a small orange pill in the corner. It stays on every screen until the timesheet is dealt with; clicking it opens the card again.

      How it would be built (for the engineer)

      All six designs share one back end: the routine's chat calls a new notify tool, the app stores the notice in notifications.json and pushes it to the window; answers go back into the source chat as one message. The full shared story is on the overview page.

      What this design adds:

      • renderer/components/notifications/ToastStack.tsx — mounted once in _app.tsx, position:fixed; right:16px; z-index:60; bottom offset = calc(var(--spawnbar-h, 0px) + 16px). SpawnBar.tsx publishes --spawnbar-h on document.documentElement via a ResizeObserver and clears it on unmount, so other screens get 16 px.
      • Max three visible, newest on top; the rest wait in a queue (FIFO by createdAt). action notices always take a slot first.
      • info: 300 px toast, removed after 6000 ms (paused while hovered), progress bar driven by a CSS animation. warn: 300 px toast with ✕ → dismissNotification(id).
      • StickyActionCard.tsx (420 px, orange left edge, no close button): pages = files → questions → to-dos/send/dismiss; progress = opened files + answered questions + ticked to-dos. Every Next calls answerNotificationQuestion, so answers survive a restart.
      • Fold state: localStorage["pf.notif.folded." + id] (try/catch); folded = the pill ● N needs you ▸, N = open action notices.
      • Dismiss button disabled until canDismiss(n) && n.answersSentAt; its title lists what is missing. The routine run stays attention until then.
      • Opening the file reuses the existing open_file route; “Open chat ›” navigates to /session?id=<conversationId>.

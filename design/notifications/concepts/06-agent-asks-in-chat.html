      How it would be built (for the engineer)

      Shared part (same for all six designs): the routine's chat calls a new notify tool on the existing peersflow server; the app stores the notification in notifications.json and pushes it to the window. Answers go back into the source chat through sendNotificationAnswers, and Dismiss is refused until canDismiss() is true. Full story: the shared build notes.

      What this design adds:

      • renderer/components/notifications/AgentAsksCard.tsx — rendered by pages/session.tsx above the terminal container whenever an open notification has source.conversationId === conv.id. The terminal keeps its size; the card takes a top band (scrolls on its own if it grows past ~45% of the height) and collapses the questions once the answers are sent.
      • renderer/components/notifications/NeedsYouChip.tsx — in the home/routines header (left of the Chat|Routines pill) and in .pf-session-header-actions. Main number = open action notices; secondary counts for open warn and live info. Click → open the oldest waiting chat (openConversation(source.conversationId)).
      • PinnedRow.tsx — a derived needsYou flag (no store change): orange badge “needs you · N questions” and the row highlight; on arrival the existing row-just-added flash is re-triggered. warn rows get a badge with ✕ (dismiss); info rows a badge with a 6 s shrinking bar.
      • 6 s pop-up: one more consumer of the existing Routines toast line, with an “Open ›” action; only for action.
      • info/warn are also written into their own chat as a muted system line (renderer-only, never sent to the model).
      • After sendNotificationAnswers, the answer text is visible in the chat as the user's message; the agent's reply comes from the agent itself, nothing is faked.
      • Persistence: nothing beyond C11 — readAt is stamped when the card first renders open; the routine run stays attention until dismissal.

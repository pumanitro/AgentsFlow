Bell + drawer

A bell sits at the top of every screen. When something needs you, a panel slides in from the right over whatever you are doing, and you answer the agent's questions right there.

  • Where it lives A bell at the top of every screen, plus a side panel
  • How you answer Inside the side panel, without leaving your screen
  • How it gets your attention The panel opens by itself; small pop-ups for the rest

      Clicks were counted by hand on the picture above, answering the four questions without the "Fill sample answers" shortcut. Typing the one written answer counts as a single click into the box. "Interruption" is a 1–5 judgement: 1 is a quiet number, 5 takes over the screen.

      How it would be built (for the engineer)

      All six designs share one back end: the agent calls a new notify tool, the main process stores the notice in notifications.json and pushes it to the window, and answers go back into the source chat as a message. The full shared story is on the overview page.

      What this design adds on top:

      • renderer/components/notifications/NotificationBell.tsx — rendered in three headers: pages/index.tsx right cluster (left of the Chat | Routines pill), pages/session.tsx before ⑂ Fork, and pages/preview.tsx. Orange count = open action notices; a yellow dot = any open warn.
      • NotificationDrawer.tsx mounted once in pages/_app.tsx: position:fixed, right, below the header, z-60, 400 px, no backdrop (the page behind stays clickable), Esc and ✕ close it. Sections "Needs you" (expanded NotificationCards with file chip, questions, to-dos, Send, Dismiss) and "Earlier" (info/warn rows with ✕ and "Clear all").
      • Mini toasts: a small stack anchored under the bell for warn and info, 6000 ms with a progress bar; while the drawer is open it moves down, just left of the drawer.
      • Auto-open: the drawer opens itself when a new action notice arrives, but only when no text input or terminal has focus (otherwise it just bumps the count), so typing is never interrupted.
      • State: drawer open/closed and expanded cards are local React state, not persisted; readAt is stamped when a card is first expanded. Dismiss is disabled until canDismiss() and the answers were sent; its tooltip lists what is missing.
      • Data needed beyond the shared model: none. Hook: lib/use-notifications.ts. Effort: M.

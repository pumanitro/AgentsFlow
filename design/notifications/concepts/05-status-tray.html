Design 05 of 6

Status-bar tray

A thin bar runs along the bottom of every screen. Its right end counts what needs you; one click opens a small checklist right above it. It flashes when something arrives, but it never covers your work.

  • Where it lives A 24-pixel bar at the very bottom, on every screen
  • How you answer A step-by-step checklist in a pop-up above the bar
  • How it gets your attention The bar flashes orange with the title for 6 seconds, then shows a counter

      How it would be built (for the engineer)

      Shared by all six designs: the agent calls one new notify tool, the app stores the notice in notifications.json and pushes it to the screen; answers go back into the same chat as a message. The full story, the data shape and the dismiss rule are on the overview page.

      What this design adds:

      • renderer/components/notifications/StatusBar.tsx — a fixed 24 px bar mounted once in pages/_app.tsx, so every page gets it. Left: bridge health, working-agent count and plan usage (data already in the app). Right: the tray button with three counters from use-notifications.ts.
      • pages/index.tsx, session.tsx and preview.tsx get pb-6 (24 px) on their root so nothing hides behind the bar. On home the bar sits under the composer.
      • TrayPopover.tsx — 380 px, anchored above the tray, newest first; closes on Esc or a click outside. An action notice renders as an accordion of six rows (file · Q1–Q4 · to-do), progress done/6 in its header; answering a yes/no or choice row opens the next unfinished row.
      • Arrival: on a new action the tray segment gets a 6 s orange flash with the title inline; each info notice gets a 6 s slot in the bar's ticker (shrinking bar), then is marked dismissed. No new data beyond the shared shape; the "file opened" tick is local UI state (could become openedAt on NotificationFile if it must survive a restart).
      • Dismiss stays disabled until the shared canDismiss() passes and answersSentAt is set; its tooltip lists what is missing. warn rows have a ✕ (one click). Effort: S.
      • Optional later: move the green bridge dot from the header into the bar.
